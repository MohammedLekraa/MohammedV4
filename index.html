ABOUT ME

I am a final-year Telecommunications Engineering student at Universitat Rovira i Virgili, with a particular interest in embedded systems, firmware, electronics and communication technologies.

My academic background combines Telecommunications Engineering with a Bachelor's degree in Biomedical Engineering. This multidisciplinary path has given me experience across electronics, programming, signal processing and the development of technological systems, while allowing me to progressively focus my career towards hardware and embedded engineering.

Through Telecommunications Engineering, I am developing a deeper understanding of digital communications, RF systems, antennas, radio propagation, radar, electromagnetic compatibility, transmitters and receivers and optical communications. At the embedded level, I am particularly interested in how microcontrollers, firmware, electronics and communication interfaces work together inside a complete physical system.

I enjoy engineering problems where the boundary between hardware and software disappears. I like working close to the physical system: interfacing sensors, writing firmware, debugging electronics, designing PCBs and understanding why a system behaves the way it does. This is also what has driven my interest in automotive electronics, embedded systems and the technology behind modern vehicles.

My interests extend from embedded firmware and electronics to RF, antennas, radar, RFID, signal processing, connected devices and robotics. I am especially interested in roles where these technologies converge and where I can contribute to building reliable systems from the hardware level upwards.

I am currently looking for opportunities where I can continue developing as an engineer in Embedded Systems, Firmware, Electronics, Hardware or Communication Systems, including applications within automotive and other technology-driven industries.
