ABOUT ME.

01 // ENGINEERING APPROACH

I like understanding how things work — and building them.

I am a Telecommunications Engineering student at Universitat Rovira i Virgili, building on a previous degree in Biomedical Engineering.

My engineering path has gradually moved toward the intersection of electronics, embedded software and communications. I am particularly interested in systems where code does not exist in isolation, but interacts directly with sensors, electronics and the physical world.

Telecommunications engineering is allowing me to deepen that foundation through areas such as radio frequency, antennas, propagation, radar, digital communications, electromagnetic compatibility and embedded communication systems.

I am especially interested in applying these technologies to automotive electronics, connected devices, robotics and intelligent embedded systems.
